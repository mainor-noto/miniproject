Eat Like a Distance Runner

What you do after practice matters as much as the workout. Smart fueling helps you feel stronger at every meet.

Daily Basics

  • Carbs: rice, pasta, bread, fruit
  • Protein: chicken, beans, eggs, yogurt
  • Fats: nuts, avocado, olive oil
  • Hydration all day, not just at practice

Pre-Run Ideas

1 hours before: bagel with peanut butter, banana, or oatmeal. Keep it simple and easy to digest.

Post-Run Recovery

Within 30 60 minutes: carbs + protein (chocolate milk, sandwich, rice chicken). Sleep 8+ hours whenever possible.
