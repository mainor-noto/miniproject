Turn Your Work into Results

Race day is about execution: smart pacing, positioning, and belief.

Pre-Race Routine

  1. Arrive 60–90 minutes early
  2. Course walk-through with your team
  3. 15 min warm-up jog + drills
  4. Put on spikes & 4–6 strides

During the Race

Start controlled, stay relaxed the first kilometer, then move up. Use hills to surge and key off teammates to maintain pace.

Mindset

Focus on one runner at a time. When it hurts, remind yourself: you trained for this moment.
