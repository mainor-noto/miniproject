Run Light, Run Fast

The right gear keeps you comfortable and confident on any course.

Training Shoes

Choose cushioned daily trainers that match your foot type. Rotate pairs to reduce injury risk and extend shoe life.

XC Spikes

Use 1/4or 3/8 spikes depending on conditions. Spikes give you grip on mud, grass, and hills.

Race Day Essentials

  • Uniform warm-ups
  • Spikes extra laces
  • Water bottle
  • Weather-appropriate layers
