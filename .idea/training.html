Build Endurance the Right Way

Good XC seasons are built from consistent mileage, easy days, and smart workouts. Avoid doing every run hard. Your future self will thank you.

Weekly Structure

  • Easy aerobic runs
  • Tempo run / threshold workout
  • Interval / hill session
  • Long run
  • 2 active recovery days

Sample Workout

Threshold Session:

  1. 10 min warm-up jog
  2. Dynamic stretches drills
  3. 5 min tempo w/ 1 min jog
  4. 6 relaxed strides
  5. 10 min cool-down

Form Strength

Focus on quick, light steps, relaxed shoulders, and driving your arms straight. Add basic strength: squats, lunges, core, and calf raises 2 3x a week.
